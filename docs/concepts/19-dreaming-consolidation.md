# Memory Consolidation / Dreaming (Concept)

> **What & Why:** Lyra consolidates memory during idle time, modeled on REM sleep. It reviews past conversations, merges duplicates, resolves contradictions, and surfaces cross-session patterns — without modifying originals.

## Mental Model

Your brain doesn't just store memories — it reorganizes them while you sleep. Lyra does the same. After a session ends or during idle, an observer process scans recent memories, finds related ones, merges near-duplicates, flags contradictions, and produces a reorganized memory bank for your review. You accept or reject. Originals are never touched.

## Key Concepts

- **AutoDream pattern:** Scan → Dedup → Resolve Contradictions → Trim Outdated → Discover Patterns → Produce Reviewable Bank [Anthropic, 2026]
- **Field-Theoretic:** Memories as continuous PDE-governed fields diffusing through semantic space. +116% F1 [Mitra, 2602.21220v1]
- **FORGE Broadcast:** Population propagation of best memories. 1.7-7.7× reward improvement [2605.16233]
