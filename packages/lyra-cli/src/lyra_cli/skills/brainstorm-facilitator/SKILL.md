# Brainstorm Facilitator

## Divergent Thinking Techniques

| Technique | How It Works | Duration |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Brainwriting** | Each person writes 3 ideas in 5 min, passes to next person | 15-20 min |
| **SCAMPER** | Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to use, Eliminate, Reverse | 20-30 min |
| **Random Stimulus** | Introduce a random word/image and force connections to the problem | 10-15 min |
| **Worst Possible Idea** | Generate deliberately bad ideas to uncover assumptions | 10 min |

## Convergent Thinking Techniques

- **Dot Voting**: Each participant gets N dots to allocate to favorite ideas
- **Impact-Effort Matrix**: Plot ideas on axes of impact vs. implementation effort
- **NUF Test**: Score each idea on New, Useful, Feasible (1-5 scale)

## Ideation Frameworks

- **Design Thinking**: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test
- **TRIZ**: Identify contradictions and apply 40 inventive principles
- **Six Thinking Hats**: Parallel thinking with role-based perspectives (White=Data, Red=Feeling, Black=Caution, Yellow=Optimism, Green=Creativity, Blue=Process)

## Facilitation Guide

- **Timeboxing**: Set strict limits per phase; use a visible timer
- **Parking Lot**: Capture off-topic ideas for later, do not discuss them now
- **Energy Management**: Alternate high-energy and low-energy activities
- **Yes, And**: Build on others' ideas before evaluating them

## Psychological Safety Principles

- Defer judgment during divergent phases
- No idea is too small or too wild to capture
- Separate idea generation from idea evaluation
- Encourage dissent and counterpoints as productive

## Innovation Prompt Library

- "What would we do if we had unlimited resources?"
- "How would a startup with no legacy constraints solve this?"
- "What is the most absurd version of this solution?"
- "What would our competitor do if they had our roadmap?"
