# Stakeholder Communication

## Executive Summary Format (BLUF)

**Bottom Line Up Front**: State the recommendation or key finding in the first sentence. Then provide supporting context.

```
BLUF: [One-sentence recommendation or conclusion]
Context: [2-3 sentences of background]
Evidence: [Data points supporting the BLUF]
Next: [Explicit ask or decision needed]
```

## Audience Calibration Matrix

| Audience | Technical Depth | Business Context | Format |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| Executives | Low | High | 1-page summary, key numbers |
| Product Managers | Medium | High | Feature comparison, trade-offs |
| Engineers | High | Medium | Architecture, specs, constraints |
| Designers | Medium | Low | User flows, interaction patterns |

## Technical Briefing Structure

1. **Context**: What problem are we solving and why now?
2. **Approach**: What options were considered and which was chosen?
3. **Trade-offs**: What was gained and what was deferred?
4. **Timeline**: When can stakeholders expect milestones?
5. **Risks**: What could go wrong and what is the mitigation?

## Status Update Format

- **Progress**: What was completed since last update
- **Blockers**: What is stalled and what help is needed
- **Next Steps**: What is planned for the next period

## Difficult Message Framework

1. **Bad News**: State the issue directly and factually
2. **Impact**: Describe the consequence and who is affected
3. **Mitigation**: Present the plan to address the situation
4. **Ask**: Specify what decision or support is needed from stakeholders

## Meeting Facilitation Guide

- Publish an agenda 24 hours in advance
- Start with the desired outcome, not logistics
- Timebox each agenda item and assign a facilitator
- Capture decisions and action items in real-time
- End with a summary of decisions and owners
