id: divergent-thinking
name: Divergent Thinking
description: Generate many diverse ideas before converging: quantity over quality, defer judgment.
keywords:
  - brainstorm
  - ideation
  - divergent
  - ideas
  - creative
  - generate
---

1. Define the challenge as a "How might we..." question. Make it specific enough to generate ideas, broad enough for creativity.
2. Set a quantity goal (e.g., 20 ideas in 10 minutes). Quantity first, quality later. Defer all judgment.
3. Use prompts to break fixation: "What would [company/person] do?", "What if budget were unlimited?", "What if we had to ship tomorrow?", "What's the opposite approach?"
4. Encourage wild ideas — they often spark practical ones from others. Build on others' ideas ("yes, and...").
5. Capture everything without filtering. No "that won't work because..." during divergent phase.
6. After hitting the quantity goal, do a quick pass: group related ideas, eliminate exact duplicates.
7. Move to convergent thinking (separate skill) to evaluate and select.
