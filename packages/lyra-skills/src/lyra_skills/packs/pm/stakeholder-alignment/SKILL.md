1. Map stakeholders: who decides, who influences, who needs to know? RACI matrix (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed).
2. Establish a decision log: what was decided, by whom, when, why, what alternatives were considered.
3. Write status updates that surface DECISIONS NEEDED, not just progress. "On track" is useless without context.
4. Escalate early: if a risk becomes likely or a dependency is blocked, raise it immediately — don't wait for the weekly update.
5. Manage expectations: under-promise and over-deliver. Never say "we're 90% done" — say what's actually working.
6. Celebrate wins publicly, discuss problems privately. Build trust through transparent communication about failures.
7. Close the loop: after a decision, confirm all stakeholders understand and accept it. Silence ≠ agreement.
