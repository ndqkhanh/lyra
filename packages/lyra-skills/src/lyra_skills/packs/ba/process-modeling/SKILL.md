name: Process Modeling
description: Model business processes using BPMN, flowcharts, or value stream mapping.

---

1. Identify the process boundaries: what triggers it, what's the end state, who participates?
2. Map the current state (as-is): observe the actual process, not what the documentation says. Include wait times and handoffs.
3. Identify bottlenecks, redundancies, and manual steps that could be automated. Quantify the waste (time, cost, errors).
4. Design the future state (to-be): what changes? What's the expected improvement? Be specific about metrics.
5. Validate with process participants: "here's what we observed, here's the proposed change — will this actually work for you?"
6. Define the transition plan: can we change incrementally or must it be a cutover? What's the rollback plan?
7. Document in a standard notation (BPMN for formal, flowchart for informal). Store where stakeholders can access.
