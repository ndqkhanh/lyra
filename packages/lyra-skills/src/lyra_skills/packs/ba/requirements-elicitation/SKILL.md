name: Requirements Elicitation
description: Gather and document business requirements through structured interviews and analysis.
keywords:
  - requirements
  - elicitation

  - stakeholder

---

1. Prepare: research the domain, review existing documentation, identify key stakeholders.
2. Conduct structured interviews: open-ended questions first ("tell me about your process"), then specific ("what happens when X fails?").
3. Distinguish needs from wants: "what problem does this solve?" If the answer is vague, drill deeper.
4. Document requirements as user stories with acceptance criteria. Each story must be independently testable.
5. Prioritize with MoSCoW: Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have (this time).
6. Validate: review requirements with stakeholders. "If we build exactly this, will it solve your problem?"
7. Trace requirements to business objectives. Every requirement should connect to a measurable business outcome.
