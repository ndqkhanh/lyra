1. Define the research question precisely — what exactly are you trying to answer?
2. Search across multiple sources: arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, conference proceedings (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, etc.).
3. Filter: read titles → abstracts → introductions → full papers. Track inclusion/exclusion with reasons.
4. Categorize findings: by method type, by result, by year, by venue quality (A* vs workshop).
5. Synthesize: what are the converging findings? Contradictions? Gaps? Open problems?
6. Write a structured review: problem definition, taxonomy of approaches, comparative analysis, gap analysis, future directions.
7. Maintain a citation graph: which papers cite which, what's the lineage of key ideas?
